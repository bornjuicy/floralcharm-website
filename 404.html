Preserved, soap or fresh?

  1. Preserved.

    Lasts
    Many months, often a year or more indoors
    Water
    None
    Best for
    Anniversaries, keepsakes

  2. Soap.

    Lasts
    Many months, kept dry
    Feel
    Velvety, rich colour, light scent
    Best for
    Big statement bouquets, gifts

  3. Fresh.

    Lasts
    About a week
    Water
    Fresh water every day or two
    Best for
    Birthdays, events, same-day
